# Attack
- A malicious act intended to compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of an organization’s data or systems. [^1]
- Deliberate Harm [^1]
- Small businesses are prime targets for these attacks, given the perception that they may have weaker defenses. [^1]
- Prevention through the use of strong, unique passwords, the use of firewalls and intrusion prevention systems, and the continuous maintenance of systems and software. [^1]
- Identification through monitoring network traffic for unusual patterns and regularly checking system logs for signs of compromise. [^1]
- Remediation through isolating affected systems, removing malicious code, and promptly reporting the incident to authorities and cybersecurity experts. [^1]
