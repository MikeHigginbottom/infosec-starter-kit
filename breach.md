# Breach
- A confirmed incident where an unauthorized actor gains access to data, applications, network, or devices which results in information being stolen or leaked. [^1] #glossary 
- Unwelcome Intrusion [^2]
- The unauthorized access, disclosure, or acquisition of sensitive data. [^2]
- It signifies a compromise of confidentiality, integrity, or availability. [^2]
- Prevention through robust access controls, regular audits of user access, and encryption of sensitive data. [^2]
- Identification through intrusion detection systems and diligently monitoring system logs for unusual activity. [^2]
- Remediation through isolating affected systems, removing unauthorized access, and promptly notifying affected parties to minimize damage. [^2]

- See also [Event Severity Scale](event-severity-scale.md)
- Also known as [Exposure](exposure.md)
